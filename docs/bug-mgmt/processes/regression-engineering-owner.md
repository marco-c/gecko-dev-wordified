#
Regression
Engineering
Owner
(
REO
)
Every
release
has
an
assigned
Regression
Engineering
Owner
(
REO
)
.
*
*
Time
commitment
*
*
:
1
hour
a
week
for
the
meeting
plus
1
hour
a
week
for
skimming
bugs
in
Bugzilla
over
the
entire
duration
of
the
version
from
Nightly
to
Release
(
usually
8
weeks
)
.
Being
able
to
attend
the
weekly
regression
triage
meeting
is
a
requirement
of
being
an
REO
.
